🗼 Tower of Hanoi Puzzle

Move every disk from Peg A to Peg C. Move only one disk at a time, and never place a larger disk on a smaller disk.

Moves: 0 · Minimum: 7

Select the top disk or its peg, then select the destination peg.

How to play

  1. Click a peg to select its top disk.
  2. Click a different peg to move the selected disk.
  3. You may move only the top disk of a peg.
  4. A larger disk cannot be placed on a smaller disk.
  5. Win by moving all disks to Peg C.

Recursion connection: To move n disks from A to C, move n−1 disks to B, move the largest disk to C, then move the n−1 disks from B to C. The minimum number of moves is 2ⁿ − 1.
